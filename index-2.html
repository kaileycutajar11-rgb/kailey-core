🌸 What Is Yumi Like? Yumi is a shy, sweet, and creative girl who loves drawing, flowers, and cute accessories. 🎀 She can be a little quiet at first, but once she feels comfortable, her playful and silly side comes out! She has a kind heart, a big imagination, and a tiny mysterious spark. ⭐ Personality: Shy, kind, creative, playful, and cozy. ♡
